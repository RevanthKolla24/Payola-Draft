Apple Music preview

This is not your full listening history. Returned items do not establish repeated plays, playback timestamps, actual listening duration, or royalty earnings. We do not convert this list into play counts, monthly charts, or money. No date-range filter is offered.

After you select “Prepare Apple connection,” this page loads Apple’s MusicKit SDK. Select “Authorize with Apple” to open Apple’s authorization flow. Your Music User Token goes directly from this browser to Apple’s API, not to our server. The Apple SDK may use its own browser storage and cookies. This app does not persist the returned list or tokens. Disconnect asks MusicKit to revoke local authorization; manage app access in Apple’s account controls if necessary.

Developer credentials are required. No Apple requests are made until you choose to prepare the connection.

Owner setup instructions

Create a MusicKit-enabled key and developer token through your Apple Developer account. Keep the signing private key outside this project. Configure APPLE_MUSIC_DEVELOPER_TOKEN on the server, then restart it. The signed developer token is intentionally delivered to MusicKit in the browser; the private signing key must never be delivered. Apple validates the token and determines account eligibility. Use HTTPS for deployment. No credentials are bundled with this prototype.
