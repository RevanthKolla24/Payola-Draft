A NOTE ON THE NUMBERS

Less mystery.
More transparency.

Listening is personal. Royalty math is complicated.
Here’s exactly what we do—and don’t—know.

How is the estimate calculated?＋

Included plays × $0.003. We use Duetti’s 2024 Spotify sample average of $3 per 1,000 streams, not a fixed Spotify rate. Its global independent-catalog sample is not representative of every artist, market, plan, or period. Read the benchmark ↗

Does the artist actually receive this amount?＋

No. This is an illustrative estimate associated with recordings, not confirmed earnings or artist take-home. Spotify uses streamshare, not a fixed per-play rate. Label, distributor, and collaborator agreements affect what artists receive. Track eligibility, including Spotify’s global 1,000-stream threshold, cannot be established from one fan’s history. Spotify’s royalties guide ↗

What’s included in my receipt?＋

For imports, we include dated Spotify music-track records with artist and title metadata and at least 30 seconds played. We exclude short plays, non-track content, incomplete metadata, and invalid records. These are not verified royalty-earning streams. Artist attribution follows the file; collaborator splits are not inferred. Manual counts are self-reported.

What happens to my listening history?＋

Spotify files are parsed locally. No history is uploaded unless you explicitly choose Save current history to account. Cloud saving sends normalized records, dates and source declarations to Firebase under your shared account. Guest mode needs no account. Last.fm mode sends your requested username and dates through our server to Last.fm, without saving history. Only an explicitly remembered Last.fm username and an optionally remembered Firebase sign-in are persisted on this device; use Forget saved username to remove it. Hosting infrastructure may log requests. Refreshing or clearing data discards imported history. Downloaded receipts are saved only when you choose. No analytics, external fonts, or artist-image requests are used. Firebase is contacted only after choosing Connect account service. Deleting cloud Payola history leaves a small version marker and empty bounded storage slots for conflict safety; it does not delete jobs or the shared account.
