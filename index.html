A NOTE ON THE NUMBERS

Less mystery.
More transparency.

Listening is personal. Royalty math is complicated.
Here’s exactly what we do—and don’t—know.

How is the estimate calculated?＋

Included plays × $0.003. We use Duetti’s 2024 Spotify sample average of $3 per 1,000 streams, not a fixed Spotify rate. Its global independent-catalog sample is not representative of every artist, market, plan, or period. Read the benchmark ↗

Does the artist actually receive this amount?＋

No. This is an illustrative estimate associated with recordings, not confirmed earnings or artist take-home. Spotify uses streamshare, not a fixed per-play rate. Label, distributor, and collaborator agreements affect what artists receive. Track eligibility, including Spotify’s global 1,000-stream threshold, cannot be established from one fan’s history. Spotify’s royalties guide ↗

What’s included in my receipt?＋

For imports, we include dated Spotify music-track records with artist and title metadata and at least 30 seconds played. We exclude short plays, non-track content, incomplete metadata, and invalid records. These are not verified royalty-earning streams. Artist attribution follows the file; collaborator splits are not inferred. Manual counts are self-reported.

What happens to my listening history?＋

Spotify files are parsed locally. No history is uploaded unless you explicitly choose Save current history to account. Separately, confirmed collection saves upload receipt summaries, titles and notes, not raw history. Cloud saving sends normalized records, dates and source declarations to Firebase under your shared account. Guest mode needs no account. Last.fm mode sends your requested username and dates through our server to Last.fm, without saving history. Only an explicitly remembered Last.fm username and an optionally remembered Firebase sign-in are persisted on this device; use Forget saved username to remove it. Hosting infrastructure may log requests. Refreshing or clearing data discards imported history. Downloaded receipts are saved only when you choose. No analytics, external fonts, or artist-image requests are used. Firebase is contacted only after choosing Connect account service. Deleting cloud Payola history leaves a small version marker and empty bounded storage slots for conflict safety; it does not delete jobs or the shared account.

Help & Privacy

Which files can I import?

Spotify Extended Streaming History audio JSON or ZIP files, and user-mapped CSV listening events. CSV works with self-reported Spotify, Apple Music or YouTube Music sources but is always unpriced. Native Apple privacy exports and YouTube Takeout are not yet supported. Playlists, liked songs and libraries do not establish plays; general watch activity must not be treated as music listening.

What if an import fails or I cancel?

Existing history stays unchanged until you confirm the review. Review per-file errors: only successful files are staged. Cancel discards the pending selection. Add checks matching playback occurrences across files; Replace starts a new history only after confirmation. CSV matching uses date, artist, title and duration, so date-only exports cannot distinguish all listening events reliably.

Where does my data go?

File parsing stays on your device. Optional Last.fm requests send username and dates through this server. Signing in contacts Firebase/Google but never uploads history automatically. Only an explicit history save uploads normalized history. Confirmed collection saves separately upload receipt summaries and notes. Guest history is temporary: download a session before leaving; CSV summaries and images are not full backups. Session files contain listening history—keep them private.

Accounts, deletion and troubleshooting

Payola Reciept and Job Tracker share a login, not listening and job data. Password changes affect both apps. Deleting Payola cloud history does not delete your account or jobs. Shared-account deletion is not offered here. For import problems, check the file feedback and column names, try a smaller batch, or unzip Spotify files first. No support address is currently published; do not share passwords, tokens or raw history in public reports.
